A home from home in the hills

A family villa with a heated pool near Málaga, set high above the Guadalhorce valley.

Villa Madrid is a traditional whitewashed Andalusian villa on its own hilltop, 2.9 km from Coín, a proper market town that hasn't been taken over by tourism. Electric gates open onto a private plot with views in every direction, so you can watch the sun come up over the town and go down behind the mountains.

The heated pool is the heart of the place: 10 × 5 m, 1.1 to 1.8 m deep, with sunbeds, balls, toys and lilos, and it can be fenced off from the garden for little ones. Around it are front and rear terraces for eating outside, a covered porch, a built-in barbecue, a pool table and darts.

Inside you'll find high beamed ceilings, free Wi-Fi, air-conditioning that also heats, an authentic Spanish lounge with a log fire for winter, a smart TV with soundbar and Xbox, and a fully equipped kitchen and utility room. We leave a welcome pack for your arrival.

Coín, your local town

Coín is a real working Andalusian town rather than a resort: whitewashed streets, tiled fountains, shaded plazas full of cafés, and the historic church of San Juan Bautista. The town is ringed by orange groves, and the Río Grande nearby is lovely for a riverside walk.

Good to know

Planning your stay

Quick answers to the questions guests ask us most.

How far is Villa Madrid from Málaga airport?

About 35 to 45 minutes by car, depending on traffic. The roads are easy, and the last stretch up to the villa is a steep private drive.

Is there Wi-Fi?

Yes, free Wi-Fi is included throughout the villa.

Is the pool heated?

Yes. The private pool is 10 × 5 m, 1.1 to 1.8 m deep and heated, so it's warm enough for spring and autumn swims. Heating is included at no extra charge. The pool area can be fenced off from the garden for young children.

Is there air-conditioning?

Yes. All three bedrooms and the lounge have air-conditioning, and the same units heat as well, so the villa stays warm in winter too.

Do I need a car?

Yes, we recommend one. Coín is 2.9 km away (a 20 to 25 minute walk into town), but the walk back up is steep, and the beaches, golf courses and Málaga are all a short drive. There's free private parking behind the electric gates.

How far is the beach?

The nearest beaches at Fuengirola and Mijas Costa are around 30 minutes' drive. Marbella and Puerto Banús are about 40 minutes.

What is there to do nearby?

Coín has plazas, cafés and tapas bars, and walks along the Río Grande. Alhaurín Golf is 16 minutes away and Lauro Golf 19 minutes. Mijas, Marbella and Málaga are easy drives, and guests have enjoyed day trips to Ronda, Gibraltar, Seville, Granada and Córdoba.

Where are the nearest shops and restaurants?

Coín has large supermarkets, shops and plenty of restaurants a few minutes' drive from the villa. Many local restaurants close on Mondays, so it's worth checking before you head out.

Is Villa Madrid good for families?

Very. There are pool toys and inflatables, board games, books and a games console, and the pool area can be fenced off. It sleeps up to 6 in three bedrooms.

Can you provide a cot or high chair?

Yes. We have a travel cot and a high chair. Just mention them in your booking request and we'll have them ready.

Are bed linen and towels provided?

Yes. Beds are made up for your arrival, and we provide bath towels plus beach and pool towels.

Is there a washing machine?

Yes. The utility room has a washing machine and a tumble dryer.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 4pm and check-out is by 11am. If your flights don't fit, ask us and we'll help where we can.

Is the villa private and secure?

Yes. Villa Madrid stands on its own hilltop plot behind electric gates, with no neighbours overlooking the pool or terraces.

Is Villa Madrid good for a winter stay?

Yes. Winters on the Costa del Sol are mild and sunny, the air-conditioning in every bedroom and the lounge also heats, and the lounge has a log fire for cooler evenings.

Who do I contact during my stay?

Trefor looks after every booking himself and replies quickly, and our caretakers live five minutes from the villa if you need help in person.

Can I bring my dog?

Sorry, pets aren't allowed at Villa Madrid.

How do I book?

Choose your dates on the calendar and send a booking request. Trefor will reply within 24 hours with a price. Booking direct with us costs less than booking through holiday websites.

Is there a minimum stay?

Yes, the minimum stay is 7 nights.

How do payment and cancellation work?

A 25% deposit secures your dates, and the balance is due 60 days before you arrive. Payment is by bank transfer and there's no security deposit. If you cancel up to 30 days before arrival, you get a full refund.
